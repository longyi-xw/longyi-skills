# Lifestyle & Travel Planning Presets

Use these presets when generating prompts for travel itineraries, personal schedules, or lifestyle routines. The user can customize these defaults at any time.

## 1. Pacing & Energy Balance (节奏与体力分配)

- **Anti-Special-Forces Pacing (`[拒绝特种兵打卡]`)**: Prioritize depth and comfort over cramming attractions. Limit major sightseeing transfers to 2-3 core spots per day with buffer time for rest or photography.
- **Photography & Golden Hour Windows (`[摄影与光线窗口]`)**: For scenic, snow mountain, or night-scene destinations, account for sunrise/sunset golden hours, weather contingencies, and optimal viewing angles.

## 2. Routing & Logistics Precision (交通与动线精确性)

- **Zero-Backtracking Route (`[不走回头路]`)**: Organize daily stops geographically in a continuous loop or linear progression, specifying exact transit modes, travel times, and transfer buffers.
- **Plan B Weather/Crowd Fallback (`[恶劣天气与人流备选]`)**: Provide an indoor or low-crowd alternative for key outdoor days in case of rain, altitude sickness, or peak holiday congestion.

## 3. Filtering & Output Structure (排雷与输出格式)

- **No Generic Marketing Fluff (`[去水词与防网红坑]`)**: Exclude over-commercialized tourist traps and vague adjectives. Provide actionable timings, ticket booking lead times, and local dining categories.
- **Actionable Table Delivery (`[结构化执行清单]`)**: Format the generated travel prompt so the execution agent outputs a day-by-day timeline table, packing/preparation checklist, and budget breakdown.
